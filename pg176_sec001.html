Invitation letter from Imma to Raheem for a birthday party at Tabata Relini on Saturday, 12th November at 2:00 pm, asking guests to wear blue, RSVP by 15th November, and followed by six comprehension questions about the invitation.
Dear Raheem,
I am planning a birthday party and I want you to be part of the fun.
Date: Saturday, 12th November
Time: 2:00 pm
Place: Tabata Relini
We will be serving different snacks, drinks and cake. So, don’t plan to miss. Wear anything you feel comfortable in, but make sure it is blue. Your presence is the best present, but if you’d like to bring a gift, please bring me some flowers.
Please RSVP by 15th November at the mobile number 0075090000 to help us plan for food and drinks.
I can’t wait to see you.
Your friend,
Imma.
