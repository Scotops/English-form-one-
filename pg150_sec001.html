The underlined sentences are in the present progressive tense or present continuous tense. This tense is used to talk about actions happening right now or around this very moment. In this tense, we use a form of the verb “to be” (am, is, are) and add the main verb with an -ing ending. For example, “I am reading a book” means I am reading the book at this moment. It is also used to describe temporary actions or plans for the future. Here are five simple sentences to show its uses.

1. She is playing with her toys.
2. They are watching TV tonight.
3. I am studying English now.
4. He is meeting his friend after school.
5. We are going to the park tomorrow.
