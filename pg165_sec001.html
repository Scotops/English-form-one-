Printed book page 159. (a) Read the following dialogue and then answer the questions that follow. [One Saturday evening, Mnaya and his friend Hida met at the school cafeteria for refreshments. While having some drinks, they shared various issues.] Mnaya: Hida, I’ve been struggling with my writing lately. It just doesn’t seem to flow the way I want it to. Hida: Writing a text is like preparing a special meal. You need a good recipe to cook delicious food. Similarly, in writing a good text, you need to follow some steps. Mnaya: What are they? Hida: Okay! The stages are prewriting, drafting, revising, editing and proofreading. Mnaya: Let me guess [scratching his head]. Prewriting is like collecting ingredients for my special meal. Hida: Exactly! In writing, prewriting involves gathering the materials, organising your thoughts and planning your writing. This is the foundation for your writing. Mnaya: Could drafting be like when I start cooking my special meal? Hida: That’s it! Drafting involves putting your ideas in written form. Just let your creativity flow and get the words down on paper as a professional chef’s first attempt at preparing a meal. Mnaya: Right! The next step is like adjusting the flavours and spices in a meal to make it test good, right? Hida: There you are! This is like revising in writing where you go back and check your draft critically just like what the chef does to improve the taste of the meal. It involves reviewing and improving the draft. This should be followed by editing which involves polishing the writing to make it error-free! Student’s Book Form One English for Secondary Schools Return to the page image
