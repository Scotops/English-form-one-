The words that have been replaced in the sentences and passages are called nouns and the new words are called pronouns. Pronouns are used to replace nouns that have already been mentioned or nouns that do not need to be specifically named. They help to communicate effectively and avoid repetition in speech and writing. The chart that follows summarises the types, definitions and examples of pronouns.
