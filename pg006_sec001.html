Preface This textbook, English for Secondary Schools, has been written specifically for Form One students in the United Republic of Tanzania. The book has been prepared in accordance with the 2023 English Language Syllabus for Ordinary Secondary Education, Form I-IV, issued by the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology. Some of the contents in this book have been adapted from English for Secondary Schools for Form One that was published in 2018 in accordance with the 2005 Ordinary Level Syllabus issued by the then Ministry of Education and Vocational Training (MoEVT). The book consists of ten chapters, namely Using ICT tools to search for information, Listening to various types of information, Producing short and coherent oral messages, Developing vocabulary from conversations and written texts, Using appropriate grammar and vocabulary in oral and written contexts, Reading texts for comprehension, Comprehending oral messages, Responding through oral and written communication, Using appropriate grammar and vocabulary for oral communication and Creating a variety of texts using appropriate tone and register. The book contains engaging exercises, real-life examples, and interactive activities that will help you develop the four basic language skills: listening, speaking, reading, and writing. In addition, the book will help you expand your vocabulary through words and phrases that you will use in your everyday communication. This will enable you to express yourself accurately and fluently in both oral and written forms. Additional learning resources are available in the TIE e-Library at https://ol.tie.go.tz or ol.tie.go.tz QR code for additional learning resources from the TIE e-Library.
