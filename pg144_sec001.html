(d) Study the following picture and answer the questions that follow.

Seven students in school uniform stand in a circle outside a classroom, talking together.

Questions

  1. 1. What do you see in the picture?
  2. 2. What could the conversation among the students you see in the picture be about?
Task symbol: a blue clipboard with a pen and four checked boxes.

Task

In a manageable group, make a conversation on one of the following scenarios and act out to the class.

  1. 1. A teacher holds a conversation with his/her student whose academic performance is low.

  2. 2. A police officer sees a man breaking into someone’s car, catches him and takes him to the police station for interrogation.
