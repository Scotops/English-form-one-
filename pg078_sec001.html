S/n Type Definition Pronouns
1. Personal pronouns They are used to refer to specific individuals or things.

1st person: I, we
2nd person: you
3rd person: he, she, it, they
2. Demonstrative pronouns They point to specific things or people in a sentence. this, that, these, those
3. Possessive pronouns They indicate ownership. mine, yours, his, their, hers, its, ours, theirs
4. Reflexive pronouns They are used when the subject and object of a sentence are the same person or thing. They show that the action in a sentence goes back to the same person or thing. myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves, themselves
5. Relative pronouns They introduce relative clauses, which provide more information about a noun in the sentence. who, whom, whose, which, that
6. Interrogative pronouns They are used to ask questions who, whom, whose, which, what
7. Indefinite pronouns They are used to refer unspecified or unknown people or things. everyone, someone, nobody, anything, each, few, several, all, some, none, etc.
