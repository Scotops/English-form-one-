Do you know? section heading with a large question mark DO YOU KNOW? The bolded words in the story are called sequential words, transition words or signal words. They help to create a chronological order of events or ideas in a story or piece of writing. They guide readers through a smooth and logical flow of events and make it easier for readers to follow the storyline. Study the table below containing sequential words and examples of sentences showing their usage. S/n Sequential words Examples of sentences 1. first/firstly First, she woke up early to get ready for her big adventure. 2. before Before she left, she made sure she had packed everything she might need. 3. next Next, she set out on her journey with a sense of excitement.
