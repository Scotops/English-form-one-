Responding to interview questions

(a) Read and practise the following dialogue. Then, answer the questions that follow.

[One day there was a school election at Denga Secondary School. Two days before, teachers and other members of the election committee interviewed the candidates. Follow the conversation between the committee and one of the candidates below.]

Chairperson:
Welcome to the school election committee panel!
Student:
Thank you all, good afternoon!
Panellist 1:
Good afternoon! Could you please introduce yourself?
Student:
My name is Kibibi Mita. I’m currently in Form One, majoring in Agriculture and Food Processing subjects. I’m excited about running for the students’ welfare leadership position.
Panellist 4:
Why are you interested in that position?
Student:
As a student studying agriculture and food processing, I believe I will use the knowledge to improve students’ meals and the overall dining experience.
Panellist 3:
Do you have any leadership experience?
Student:
Ooh! Yes! When I was in primary school, I was a chairperson of the Health Committee, and I was responsible for food-related issues.
Panellist 2:
Can you share with us the changes you expect to bring if you are elected?
Student:
I’ll improve the quality of our meals through the provision of a balanced diet, and I’ll ensure a safe cooking environment.
Panellist 3:
How will you manage to do so?
Student:
I’ll set goals and share them with the school management. In addition, I’ll use my knowledge of agriculture to introduce vegetable gardens in the school environment.
