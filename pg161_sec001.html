Directions to Mjimwema Market

Collins was a newcomer to Majibaridi. He wanted to go to Mjimwema Market, but he did not know the way. He asked Victoria to direct him. The following were the directions she gave him.

Simple map of the route to Mjimwema Market, showing branching roads through bush, a river crossing in the valley, a hill with a radio tower and baobab tree, and buildings along the road on the right.

“First of all, take the cattle track through the bush until you get to a dirt road on your left. Follow it until you get to where it branches. Take the right branch and walk down into the valley. Cross the river and walk straight up the hill. At the top, you will see a radio tower on your left, near a huge baobab tree. Walk past the radio tower and carry on straight for about 500 metres until you reach the village office on your right. Then, turn left and walk for another 200 metres. On your left, you will see a bus station and then Mjimwema Primary School on the same side. After the school, on the right, you will find Mjimwema Market.”
