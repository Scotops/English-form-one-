Activity 4: Using appropriate non-verbal cues in interpersonal communication Activity 4 Using appropriate non-verbal cues in interpersonal communication (a) Study the following dialogues. Then, answer the questions that follow. Dialogue 1 [Pilly arrives at Mrs Ndevu’s place to help her with cleaning her compound.] Pilly: Good morning, Mrs Ndevu! How are you today? Mrs Ndevu: Oh, hello Pilly. I am not as strong and young as I used to be. Thanks for coming over to help me with cleaning. Pilly: No problem at all, Mrs Ndevu. It’s nice to spend time with you and help you with the work. What should we start with? Mrs Ndevu: Well, let’s begin with watering those plants over there. Pilly: Okay! Let me carry this bucket of water. Mrs Ndevu: Thank you so much, Pilly. You have been very helpful. There are very few young people who behave like you nowadays. Pilly: My pleasure! Questions: 1.
