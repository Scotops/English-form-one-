(b) Study the following tips for writing an invitation and read the invitation that follows. Then, answer the questions below it.

Tips for writing an invitation

(i)

Use the present continuous tense for plans and arrangements.

(ii)

Begin with capital letters in writing days of the week, months and names of streets.

(iii)

You can use the expression ‘hope you can come’ to complete your invitation.

(iv)

RSVP is used on invitation to mean “please reply” to enable an invitee to respond if she/he will attend or not.

(v)

Use a suitable tone and language.

(vi)

Be respectful.

(vii)

Provide complete information about the events.

(viii)

Give contact information.

(ix)

Specify requirements if any.

(x)

Review the invitation before sending it.
