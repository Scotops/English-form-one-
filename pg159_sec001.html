Expressing ownership/ possession

(a) Pair up and take things from your bag and put them on your desks. Take turns asking and responding to questions about the items on the desk. An example is provided for you.

Two schoolgirls at a desk discuss ownership of a pencil, with books, pens, a ruler, and an open exercise book in front of them.

Example:

Question: Is this your pencil?

Answer: No, it isn’t mine. It’s yours.
