Phone conversations (a) Study and practise the following conversations. Then, answer the questions that follow. Conversation A Speaker 1: Hello! My name is Kongoya Malumbi! How’s it going? Speaker 2: Hello Kongoya! It’s going well. What’s up! Speaker 1: I’m sorry. I’m Londa’s classmate. May I speak with him? Speaker 2: OK. Hold on a moment. I need to check if he is available. (A moment later)! I’m very sorry, Londa is not here right now. Would you like to call back later or leave a message? Speaker 1: Could you tell him that tomorrow is my birthday? I would like to invite him at 4:00 pm to celebrate it with me. The event will take place at home. Speaker 2: OK! I will give him the message! Have a good preparation for your special day! Speaker 1: Thank you so much. Speaker 2: Take care. Bye. Speaker 1: Bye. Conversation B Mwananchi: Good afternoon. My name is Sadala Nova, from Kurunzi in Mpome Ward. Mbonile: Good afternoon. You are welcome Mr. Nova, This is Mbonile speaking from the headquarters of the National Environmental Management Council (NEMC) Public Relations and Communication Unit, how can I help you?
