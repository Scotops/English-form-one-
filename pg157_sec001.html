English for Secondary Schools Student’s Book Form One, printed page 151

Printed book page 151. A young man pulls a wooden cart loaded with travellers’ luggage near a bus at a station, with cars and buildings in the background. He wakes up very early in the morning to carry luggage for travellers. He says his work is difficult, but he manages to get enough money to live a decent life. He is now thinking of building a house. Unlike Paul Ndesa, some young men earn their living through illegal means. Such people often get into trouble with the police and end up in jail. Student’s Book Form One English for Secondary Schools Question box asking where Paul Ndesa works, what he does to earn a living, whether his job is legal, and what he plans to do with his money. Questions 1. Where does Paul Ndesa work? 2. What does he do to earn a living? 3. Is he doing a legal job? 4. What is he planning to do with his money?
