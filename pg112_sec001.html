Paraphrasing oral messages from presentations

(a) Study the following two paragraphs and answer the questions that follow.

Paragraph A

Success has different meanings to various people. You must take time to decide how you will define your success. If you do that, you’ll be aware of your objectives. Additionally, you will be able to determine whether your decisions are helping you to accomplish your goals. Establishing goals, mission and vision statements can help you focus on your most important objectives. Besides, you will highlight the opportunities you should grab.
