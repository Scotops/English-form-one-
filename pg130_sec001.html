(a) Act out the following dialogue and answer the questions that follow. [On a graduation day at Msalato High School, Mr Kabezi visits his daughter. He meets his friend Mr Mussa.] Mr Kabezi: Sophia, this is Mzee Mussa, my former classmate at Nyamgongo Primary School. Sophia: Shikamoo. It’s nice to meet you, Mzee Mussa. Mr Mussa: Marahabaa. Nice to meet you too, Sophia. Who is that pretty girl with you, Sophia? Sophia: Oh! This is my best friend, Tausi. We are in the same class. Mr Mussa: [Facing Tausi] Hello Tausi. How are you? Tausi: Hello Mr Mussa. I am okay. Nice to meet you. Mr Mussa: Nice to meet you too, Tausi. What are you going to be when you finish school? Tausi: I plan to employ myself after I finish school. Two men speak with two schoolgirls in uniform in the school compound, as one man introduces the girls to his friend. Mr Kabezi: Good! I think that is a great idea. Do you have the same idea, Sophia?
