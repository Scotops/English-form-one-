How can the steps identified in the conversation help you? (b) Listen and respond to the oral instructions given to you by your teacher. (c) Play the Simon Says game and respond to the oral instructions. (One student volunteers to be Simon. Simon stands up and faces the other students. Then, he/she provides them with some instructions to respond to. If the statement begins with “Simon says”, the class/individual must respond to the instruction. However, when the statement does not begin with “Simon says”, they should not respond. For example Open the door. Close your eyes. Clap your hands. Stand up. Open your book.
