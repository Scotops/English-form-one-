Paragraph B explains that success means different things to different people and encourages defining success personally, checking whether choices support one’s goals, and using goals, mission, and vision statements to stay focused on opportunities.

Paragraph B

Everyone has a different concept of success. You need time to define success in your terms. In that way, you will realise your focus before you are able to do so. You will also be able to recognise whether your choices assist you in achieving your objectives or not. Setting goals, mission and vision statements can help you focus on your target. Moreover, you will underscore the chances you should seek.

Questions

  1. 1. Which synonyms are used in the paraphrased version to replace the words/expressions used in the original version?
  2. 2. What other words or phrases could you use to paraphrase specific sentences or phrases in the original paragraph?
  3. 3. What do you find challenging about paraphrasing?

(b) Pair up to listen to the oral text read by your teacher. Then, paraphrase and present it to the class.
