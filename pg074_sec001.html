Activity 2: Constructing sentences using appropriate grammar and vocabulary relating to a specific context Activity 2 Constructing sentences using appropriate grammar and vocabulary relating to a specific context (a) Read the following text and take note of the italicised words. It was just recently that Ambilike discovered that he was almost running out of money. Initially, he was in a boarding school, so he did not have to buy his food for the whole year. He had just moved to an apartment and decided to go to a nearby market and buy an apple, a mango, two bananas, one kilogram of meat, and a small bag of maize flour. When he found out that the money he had could not pay for all the things he wanted, he returned the bag of maize flour and paid for the rest of the things.
