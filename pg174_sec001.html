Task

Choose any of the following situations and use the components you have studied in (b) to write a letter using appropriate tone and register.

1.Imagine you have received a gift from your mother/father/uncle/aunt who lives in a different region.Write a letter to thank him/her and explain how you are going to use or enjoy the gift.

2.You have heard that your friend has lost one of her/his parents.Write a letter to her/him to express your condolences.

3.Imagine you have a new pen pal in another country.Write a letter and introduce yourself to him/her.Tell him/her about your hobbies and favourite subjects and ask him/her about his/her life and hobbies.

4.You borrowed your friend’s textbook, unfortunately, you lost it.Write a letter to your friend to apologise for losing the book.Explain what happened and promise to replace it.
