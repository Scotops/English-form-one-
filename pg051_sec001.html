"Do you know?" heading with a large question mark. DO YOU KNOW? A debate is a discussion whereby individuals express their arguments by focusing on a specific topic or idea. Usually, in a school context, a debate involves two sides or teams: one supporting a proposition (the affirmative team), and one opposing a proposition (the opposing team). However, there should be a debate leadership to ensure the quality of evidence, argument and the performance of the debate, hence making a judgement. The leaders of a debate include a chairperson, a secretary and a timekeeper. Sometimes, in the school context, the judgement may be done by a teacher. The winner is the one who gets the highest points. The floor (audience) may be involved in the debate, but they will not be directly engaged in the discussion.
