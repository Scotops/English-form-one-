(h) Read aloud the passage below.

Zundo was a stubborn boy. He was also lazy and careless. He disobeyed his parents and disrespected his elders. Although he was selected to join secondary school, he refused to go. His parents tried to force him, but he asked them to leave him alone. At home, he did not do any chores, and he lived by stealing other people’s things. His parents had warned him that he would end up in prison, but he did not listen to them. One day, he decided to go to the shop although he did not have money. He entered the shop and stuffed a few items in his shorts. The security guards saw what he was doing and arrested him. When he was asked about what he did, he was dishonest about it. When they searched him, they found the items. As they were about to handcuff him, he managed to escape. He ran away and grabbed something else on his way out.
