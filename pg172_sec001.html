Sample friendly letter dated October 16, 2023, from Poppy in Bububu, Zanzibar, to Fetty, saying she misses her, describing boarding school life and a party she refused to attend, and looking forward to seeing her during the December holidays. P. O. Box 2312 Bububu, Zanzibar October 16, 2023 Dear Fetty, I hope this letter finds you well. It feels like ages since we last saw each other. I wanted to drop you a quick note to catch up and say how much I miss you. My school life at the boarding school has been crazy. When we first arrived early February, a party was organised in our honour. Many Form One students attended it. I could not go because you know how scared I get when I am around many people. Many students tried to convince me to go, but I refused. You could see I was right not to go as those who went said they did not have a good time there. How is everything there? I cannot wait to see you during the December holidays. Your loving friend, Poppy
