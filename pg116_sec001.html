Task

Pair up and prepare statements and orders of your choice. Then, use them to play the Simon Says game.

Responding to oral information

(a) Study and practise the following conversation:

Zia, I’ve forgotten my exercise book at home. What should I do?
I think you should ask for permission to go back for it.
