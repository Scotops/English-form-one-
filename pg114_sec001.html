Printed book page 108. Oral instructions and orders (a) Study the following conversation and respond to the questions that follow. [Sesi and Fati are friends and classmates at Nronga Secondary School. They met when they were going to the market. Here is their conversation]: Sesi: Hi Fati! Fati: Hi Sesi! Wow, you look great my friend. Sesi: Thank you, Fati! You look gorgeous too. Where are you off to? Fati: To the market. Kalali Market is far but we can’t avoid going there. Sesi: Sure. By the way, yesterday you impressed me so much. You responded to our class teacher’s instruction very well. Fati: Ooh! You know my brother who is a teacher at Mamboseni Secondary School teaches me at home. Last week, he taught me four steps in responding to oral instructions. Sesi: Really? Lucky you! What are those steps, my friend? Fati: Relax, Sesi [she clears her throat and continues] He said the steps are referred to as SBTT, which is the short form of Smile- Breathe- Think-Talk. Sesi: I got you. SBTT, right? Fati: Exactly. When you are asked a question you should smile to yourself. Smiling reduces or removes anxiety and fear and makes you feel at ease about the question. When you breathe you let go of any negative emotion about the question and give yourself time to concentrate. After breathing, think about the answer in a calm way, and finally, you talk to deliver your carefully thought answer. That’s all Sesi. Return to the page image
