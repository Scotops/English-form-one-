Task

Prepare an oral presentation about any meeting you attended at school or home by answering the following questions:

1. What was the meeting about?
2. Who called and led the meeting?
3. What ideas/opinions did you give in the meeting?
4. Do you think there was a need to hold the meeting? Give reasons.
