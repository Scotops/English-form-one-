The meeting

Last month, I attended the students’ government meeting. The meeting took place in the Form One classroom. Members of the students’ government arrived on time. After their arrival, the chairperson first confirmed the quorum before he called the meeting to order at 10. am. The secretary started writing the minutes of the meeting. Next, the chairperson asked the secretary to read the items on the agenda. Some of the items were “conserving the school environment” and “improving speaking skills among the students”. Thereafter, the members discussed each item independently. The chairperson noted the names of all the members who wished to speak and allowed them one after another. Each member took the opportunity to give his/her opinions. I was the first to give my opinion on the two aforementioned items. Firstly, I suggested that we need to plant enough trees and take care of them. This will help to protect soil erosion and conserve our school environment. In addition, I asked the chairperson to organise an event to educate all students on the importance of conserving the school environment and the impacts of destroying it. Secondly, I talked about strategies for improving speaking skills. The strategies that I proposed include encouraging all students to participate in morning talks at the assembly ground, forming an English club and conducting regular debates on given topics. All my ideas were accepted
