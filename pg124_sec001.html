Doctor: Well, you have to go for laboratory tests so that we can identify the problem. The laboratory is on your left after you get out of this room. You will come back after I have received the results. [After the results are out, the patient goes back to the doctor] Doctor: The laboratory test shows that you have a bacterial infection in your alimentary canal. Patient: Is that a serious infection Doctor? Doctor: Don’t worry, after using the prescribed medicine you will be fine. Patient: OK, Doctor. Doctor: Here is the prescription. Visit the hospital pharmacy adjacent to the reception desk. Get well soon! Patient: Thank you, Doctor.
