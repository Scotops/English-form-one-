(d) Read the passage below and answer the questions that follow. Gender equality Firstly, it is important to know that, previously, many African societies showed a high level of gender equality. Men and women contributed to their communities in various ways. However, colonialism and globalisation brought a lot of changes in many African societies. Some cultures experienced a loss of gender equality, with women and girls losing access to certain rights and opportunities they had earlier enjoyed. Later on, African governments and civil society organisations recognised the potential of women’s involvement in decision-making processes, leading to many women being involved in social, political and economic activities. Education was used to promote gender equality, especially for girls. Education for girls not only empowers them to become active participants in the workforce but also leads to more inclusive and sustainable development. Lastly, it is important to note that gender equality in Africa remains an ongoing journey. While a lot has been achieved, there are still some challenges in rural and marginalised communities. As Africans, let us move forward, ensuring that every individual, regardless of gender, has equal opportunities to flourish and contribute to a brighter and more prosperous future.
