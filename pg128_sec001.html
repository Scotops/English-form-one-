Student:

Thank you for this opportunity. I look forward to hearing from you.

Questions

1. What is the interview about?

2. What will the candidate do if she is elected?

3. Do you think the student has responded well to the questions? Why?

4. What have you learned from the interview?

5. Which leadership position do you intend to contest? Why?

(b) Imagine you are competing for an academic leadership position in your school.
In a group, use the following questions and role-play conducting an interview by taking turns of the interviewer and candidate positions.
  1. 1. What makes you think you fit in that position?
  2. 2. What will you do to help your fellow students with low academic performance?
  3. 3. How will you motivate your fellow students to study hard?
