(b) Imagine you have met your friend who is also your age mate and you have not seen each other for a long time. Write a simple conversation between the two of you. Use the following questions as you write your conversation.

  1. 1. Who is your friend? (Give your friend a name.)
  2. 2. How do you say “hello” to your friend? (Write down the greetings you use.)
  3. 3. What would you like to discuss with your friend? (Choose a simple topic like your favourite food, fun activity, or your favourite pet.)
  4. 4. What does your friend say in response? (Write what your friend might say about the topic)
  5. 5. How do you end your conversation?
