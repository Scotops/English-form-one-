Dialogue 2

[Yonda has been called to Mr Raheem’s office.]

Mr Raheem:

Yonda, have a seat, please. I’d like to talk to you about something important.

Yonda:

Sure, Mr Raheem. What’s it about?

Mr Raheem:

Yonda, I’ve received complaints from some colleagues that you have been rude to them. Can you tell me what’s happening?

Yonda:

It’s true. I apologise for that, Mr Raheem. Things have not been going well at home lately.

Mr Raheem:

I’m sorry about the situation at home but you must observe respect and discipline at work place. Hopefully, this will not happen again.

Yonda:

I’ll make sure that I improve my conduct and behave professionally.

Mr Raheem:

That’s good to hear, Yonda. Remember that we are a team and we must respect each other. If you need any assistance, don’t hesitate to see me.

Questions

1. Why was Yonda called to Mr Raheem’s office?
2. How did Yonda respond to the questions she was asked?
3. What advice did Mr Raheem give to Yonda?
4. From the conversation, what is the relationship between Yonda and Mr Raheem? Why do you think so?
