Task

Use the guidelines below to give opinions about people, places or things.

1.

Pair up and think about people, places, or things you love, like, dislike, or hate. Then, take turns asking questions and giving opinions. Use the expression “What do you think of …….” Your partner will respond to your question using personal pronouns, e.g. I like her. She is a good person.

2.

Share your work with the class for discussion.
