Printed book page 173. Student’s Book Form One English for Secondary Schools Manjana: Okay! Who can tell us the meaning of environment? Jamal: If I can chip in quickly, I believe it is everything around us - the air we breathe, the water we drink, the land we cultivate, animals, even the trees we are sitting under right now. Nyenza: I agree. Our environment consists of all things and their surroundings. So it’s important to protect it. Manjana: Why should we? Protect it, I mean. Nyenza: Our survival depends on the environment. It provides us with resources like food, clean water, and shelter. So, if we don’t stop destroying it, we’ll perish! Jamal: Yes! Our economy relies heavily on agriculture and tourism, both depend on a healthy environment. Protecting our forests, rivers and wildlife will continue to attract more tourists. Manjana: [Reflecting] I see what you mean. But I’ve heard about issues that destroy the environment. What are they? Jamal: Ummm….! There are challenges we face and the biggest of all is deforestation. People cut down trees for firewood without replanting, new ones, leading to soil erosion. Nyenza: Another challenge is pollution from industrial waste, plastic, and even the air we breathe. I think this is the biggest threat. Manjana: Well said! I have seen how soil erosion has reduced our food production. Also, the use of chemicals has contaminated the groundwater. Nyenza: So we have all these challenges, but how do we overcome them?
