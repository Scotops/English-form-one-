English for Secondary Schools Student’s Book Form One — printed page 65

I opened my eyes wide and found that I was not in the library. It was just a nightmare. The whole family was in my room, and the hands I had felt were theirs.

Questions

1. What is the story about? 2. Is the language used simple or difficult to you? Give reasons. 3. What is the meaning of the following expressions? (i) My heart skipped a beat. (ii) I felt the skin leaving my body. (iii) That scared me out of my wits. (iv) My body started to shake like a leaf on a rainy day.
