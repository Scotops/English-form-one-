A student in school uniform stands before five seated panellists, answering questions during a school leadership interview.
Panellist 4:
Is there a specific concern about food in our school?
Student:
Yes, sometimes we are served low-quality food and burnt beans. In addition, the kitchen is most of the time dirty.
Panellist 5:
What will you do to deal with students who misbehave during eating?
Student:
I will set rules and monitor their implementation accordingly. I will also introduce a public discussion on table manners.
Panellist 2:
In leadership, conflicts or disagreements can arise. How will you handle such situations?
Student:
Communication is vital for resolving conflicts. I will maintain open and transparent communication channels with all students, teachers and other staff members. If conflicts do arise, I’ll initiate discussions to understand their perspectives, concerns and suggestions about solutions. I believe that collaboration often leads to the best solutions.
