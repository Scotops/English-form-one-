Printed book page 64. A frightened boy sits at a desk with an open book while a glowing purple book floats in front of him near a bookshelf. “What are you reading?” the voice whispered. My heart skipped a beat! I jumped from my chair in fright. I felt the skin leaving my body. I wanted to scream, but I remembered that I was in the library where no noise was allowed. I looked around carefully, but I couldn’t see anyone. I felt a warm breath in my right ear. “You need to read this book” the voice whispered. Then, the book moved and stopped right in front of me and slowly rested on my table. My body started to shake like a leaf on a windy day. I needed my mother or someone at that moment. It took all the courage I had to respond. “W…hat is…in the book? I asked with a trembling voice. “Greatest life ideas! You follow this to the letter and you won’t fail!” the voice said enthusiastically. I felt a hand on my shoulder. This time I didn’t care where I was, I screamed at the top of my lungs. I felt several hands shaking me. That scared me out of my wit! I opened a corner of my right eye and looked around. I saw that I was at home in my room.
