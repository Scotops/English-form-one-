Mr Sikujua: Hello! Ms Brilliant. Nice to see you. Ms Brilliant: Hello! Nice to see you, too! Mr Sikujua: Well… I hope you’re having a wonderful time in our town. Ms Brilliant: Sure, I arrived yesterday and I already feel at home. By the way, how’s the business going in Kigoma? Can I get wholesale clients for my goods? My company deals with cosmetics from Europe and China. Mr Sikujua: Of course! My only daughter, Sophia, deals with cosmetics too. She is very famous in the three townships of Ujiji, Kasulu and Kibondo. Ms Brilliant: Oh! My visit to Kigoma has been a success! I look forward to meeting your daughter and convincing her to be the agent of my products here in Kigoma. Mr Sikujua: Alright! No problem. I’ll inform her about it tonight. Goodbye! Ms Brilliant: Goodbye! Have a great weekend, Mr Sikujua. Mr Sikujua: Thank you. Take care. Ms Brilliant: I’m sorry, Mr Sikitu for not involving you in my talk with Mr Sikujua. It was all about my business! I apologise for that. Mr Sikitu: Don’t worry, Ms Brilliant! I know how it goes. Business people are always sensitive to information related to their businesses. Ms Brilliant: Thank you for understanding, Mr Sikitu, um … I think I should leave you now. Mr Sikitu: I understand. See you tomorrow then and take care. Ms Brilliant: See you tomorrow. Mr Sikitu: Goodbye.
