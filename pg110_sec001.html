Shedenga: Thank you, my friend! You’re a friend indeed! But I have one last question. Is paraphrasing necessary? Gulila: Of course! Paraphrasing helps in developing our vocabulary and enhancing communication skills. Shedenga: [Scratching his head ] I can guess the other significance! Er, it helps in interpreting ideas and information! Gulila: Wonderful! Exactly right! Apart from all that, paraphrasing sharpens our thinking and understanding of a text or a conversation. Shedenga: Thanks Gulila! I’ve gained a lot! I promise you I’ll be a good at paraphrasing in the future. Gulila: Don’t mention my friend! Practice makes one perfect! Shedenga: Tonight, I’m going to paraphrase some sentences and you‘ll check them in the morning! Gulila: That’s okay! You’re welcome again! Shedenga: Goodnight!
