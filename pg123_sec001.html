English for Secondary Schools Student’s Book Form One, printed page 117

Doctor:

I’m fine. How can I help you?

Patient:

I‘ve not been feeling well. I’ve had pains in my stomach since morning.

Doctor:

What did you eat in the morning or at night?

Patient:

At night I had rice with vegetables for dinner. In the morning I had cassava with kachumbari for my breakfast.

Doctor:

How were these meals prepared? Did you cook them?

Patient:

No, Doctor. I just bought from food vendors in the street!

A doctor consulting a male patient across a desk in a clinic.
Doctor:

Please explain clearly how you feel.

Patient:

Continual stomach aches and headaches. Sometimes I feel like vomiting.

Doctor:

Have you taken any painkillers?

Patient:

I’ve taken nothing.
