(b) Practise the following dialogue and answer the questions that follow.

[Jontwa is my favourite cousin. We have different hobbies and interests. I prefer sports and games, but he is an indoor person. I prefer shopping at the local market, whereas she prefers shopping at the supermarket. Last week, I received a call from him. This is how the conversation was]

Jontwa:
Hi Libe.
Libe:
Hello, Jontwa. What’s up?
Jontwa:
I’m okay, but I was wondering if you could lend me some money.
Libe:
Ha ha ha! Are you serious? Wait! Jontwa, are you in trouble?
Jontwa:
No cos, I’m short of money and I’m at the checkout point here at Keila Supermarket. Help me, please.
Libe:
No. Why do you like going there when you know you’re just a pauper?
Jontwa:
That’s your opinion, Libe. What I know is that even paupers like me have the right to go to the supermarket.
Libe:
Look here! I don’t mean to be mean, but I think it is wise to shop where you can afford.
Jontwa:
I respect your opinion. In my opinion, shopping at a supermarket is better than at a local market because some things here are very cheap. Besides, this place is clean and it has a lot of goods. Oh! I forgot about the ice cream. The ice cream here is amazing!
Libe:
Oh dear! You like ice cream. Isn’t there anything else you can think of? I believe that goods in the local market are cheaper, Jontwa. You can even bargain and get more than you need. Some things are fresher compared to those sold in the supermarket. The food is organic in the supermarket.
